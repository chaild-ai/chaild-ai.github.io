We are delighted to invite you to **Celebrating Youth Voices in AI & Data Science**, a project showcase bringing together the young people, researchers, teachers and families who have shaped our work over the past year.

## Event details

- **Date:** Saturday, 26 September 2026, 10am – 4pm
- **Location:** Reuben College Dining Hall, University of Oxford
- **Host:** Child-Centred AI Design Lab, Human Centred Computing Research Group, Department of Computer Science

The morning will set the scene by exploring key opportunities and challenges concerning young people and AI, and will share the work of the Youth Advisory Group in shaping research in this area. In the afternoon, breakout discussions will give young people, teachers, and parents a chance to share their own views on AI and education.

## Who it's for

The day is designed for the whole community around this work: the young people who took part in the advisory group, the researchers who worked alongside them, teachers, and parents. The breakout sessions run as separate roundtables for each group, so there is space for teachers, youth, and parents to each dig into what matters most to them.

## Getting involved

Refreshments and lunch are provided throughout the day. If you've been invited and haven't yet let us know whether you can join, please respond to your invitation so we can plan numbers for lunch — and feel free to pass it on to a colleague who might be interested.

## Getting here

Oxford is a lovely city while driving around Oxford can be challenging. Unfortunately, we cannot offer any onsite parking and parking can be quite expensive in Oxford. Oxford also operates a traffic congestion charge for all vehicles, [see details](https://www.oxfordshire.gov.uk/transport-and-travel/oxfords-temporary-congestion-charge-cars).

We recommend the following travel means:

- By Bike: You will see plenty of cycling around Oxford as it a cycle friendly city and a great way to see the city. Reuben College has secure bike parking available onsite.
- By Train: Reuben College is a 20 minute walk or 15 min bus ride from [Oxford Railway](https://www.nationalrail.co.uk/stations/oxford/) Station with regular trains to and from London Paddington and London Marylebone stations, as well as Birmingham and Manchester.
- By Bus: The college is a 12 minute walk from Gloucester Green Bus station, which runs regular buses to and from London airports and the rest of the UK. The Oxford Tube, a London to Oxford coach service, runs 24/7. The National Express website has information about coach connections to other parts of the UK.
- By Car: If you need to travel to Reuben by car, we recommend you use the popular [Park and Ride](https://www.oxford.gov.uk/parkandridesites) system that operates from various locations on the Oxford ring road. There is limited paid parking available in Oxford city centre.
- By Airplane: If you are arriving by airplane to the UK, we recommend using public transport to transit to Oxford such as train or bus.
