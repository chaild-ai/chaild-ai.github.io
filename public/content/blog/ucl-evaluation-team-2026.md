Two new people have joined the CHAILD team at the UCL Knowledge Lab this autumn: **Yildiz Uzun**, who started in September, and **Dr Kyrill Potapov**, who started in October.

They join [Manolis, Zaki and Vid](/blog/zaki-joins-2026/) on the UCL side of the CHAILD project, and both are here to help with our final evaluation study: the last major piece of our research plan, where we will look at how the AI tool interfaces and approaches we co-designed with children actually impact their agency in practice over several weeks.

An evaluation study like this takes a lot of work — planning sessions, working with schools and young people, and continuously analysing interaction data. Having Yildiz and Kyrill on board will make a real difference as the project heads into its final stretch.
